# State-Level Sourcing Scope and Mandi Ranking Deferral

## Status
Superseded (in part) by [ADR 0006](0006-persona-expansion-and-reconciled-roadmap.md) — curated benchmark hub mandi references are permitted with coverage caveats; full mandi rankings on unstratified extracts remain deferred.

In Milestone 1, TradeAtlas restricts sourcing recommendations to State-Level Intelligence (Gujarat vs. Rajasthan benchmark price spread and aggregate seasonal arrivals) and explicitly defers individual Mandi rankings to Milestone 2. While market-level intelligence is desirable, the current pinned extracts contain aggregate state-monthly data without verified daily market-level grade stratification. Attempting to rank individual mandis on unstratified data creates a high risk of misleading procurement leads.
