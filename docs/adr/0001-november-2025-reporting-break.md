# Handling the November 2025 AGMARKNET Reporting Break

In November 2025, the Indian government upgraded AGMARKNET to AGMARKNET 2.0, significantly altering market coverage and reporting frequency. Because reported arrival volumes before and after November 2025 are not like-for-like, TradeAtlas strictly isolates pre-break and post-break periods. Direct sum or average comparisons of arrival volumes spanning across November 2025 are prohibited to prevent false seasonal signals.
